SCENE 04

Director's Commentary

Turn the commentary track on. Here's the context behind each cut in the reel — what the problem actually was, and why I built it the way I did.

00:00

TruthLens

Misinformation spreads faster than fact-checkers can keep up, so I wanted to see if an LLM could act as a first-pass filter. TruthLens takes any article and breaks it down into a credibility score, source-quality read, and flagged language patterns — the goal wasn't a black-box verdict, but a transparent reasoning trail a reader can actually check for themselves.

00:04

Movie Recommendation System

Most recommender tutorials stop at "cosine similarity on a small CSV." I pushed this one to the full MovieLens 1M dataset and cared more about the deployment constraint than the model itself — compressing the whole hybrid TF-IDF + SVD pipeline down to under 2MB so it could run instantly inside a Streamlit app instead of needing a server to do the heavy lifting.

00:08

Netflix Catalog EDA

This one was about restraint — 7,787 titles is enough data to tell any story you want if you go in with a conclusion already picked. I forced myself to let the genre and release-year patterns speak first, then wrote the findings around what the notebook actually showed, not what made the best headline.

00:12

The Dev's Coffee Stand

My first real step into Web3 — a tip jar dApp on the Stellar network, built to actually understand how testnet transactions, wallets, and smart contract calls work under the hood, not just read about them. Cleared Level 1 / White Belt with a real XLM transaction executed end to end.
